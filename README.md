### 01 / Built a real business

At Italeen, customers can buy from several independent producers in one storefront. Food is typically prepared at the producer's premises, while Italeen coordinates the sale, logistics and customer relationship.

I secured funding from **Invitalia** and a sponsored workspace at **Kilometro Rosso Innovation District**. I built and ran the business as a solo founder, handling software, producers, operations, marketing and customer support.

→ [How Italeen works](projects/italeen.md)

### 02 / Turned operations into software

A seemingly straightforward shopping cart becomes difficult when each producer ships independently, products have different VAT rates and food must show meaningful prices per kilogram or litre.

Instead of maintaining one giant custom plugin, I developed a focused WooCommerce stack:

- **Multi-Artisan Shipping** — separate shipping packages and dispatch information per producer.
- **Proportional Shipping Tax** — distribute shipping VAT across the goods in each final package without changing the customer-facing shipping charge.
- **Italian Product Compliance** — consistent unit prices and quantity facts for food, including variations.
- **Bottega Futura** — a lean GeneratePress child theme, with clear ownership between presentation and business logic.

→ [Explore the technical decisions](projects/commerce-stack.md)
