# Evidence, scope and publication policy

A portfolio should distinguish **a live outcome**, **implementation evidence** and **future ambition**.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Italeen is an operating artisan-commerce storefront | [Public website](https://italeen.com); [published terms describing its logistics model](https://italeen.com/termini-condizioni/) | Publicly observable storefront and stated operating model, not an independent audit of financial results |

| Founder helped establish Dinamic Nuoto Vallecamonica ASD | Founder-provided account | Direct association and role are not independently audited here |

## Disclosure rules

1. **Public does not mean open source.** All source code from private repositories stays private unless separately and deliberately licensed and published.
2. Do not copy secrets, environment files, access tokens, supplier contracts, personal records, private screenshots, database exports or customer order details.
3. Do not publish identifiable athlete, employee, producer or customer information without the appropriate basis and permission.
4. Label active business, public app, implementation-only features, pilots and long-term ideas distinctly.
5. Before adding numerical traction claims, verify their definitions, time period, supporting records and publication rights.
6. Any future diagram or screenshot is illustrative unless its origin, date and environment are stated.

This repository is intentionally small, static and free of telemetry, trackers and external JavaScript.
