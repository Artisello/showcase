# Building beyond software

## Dinamic Nuoto Vallecamonica ASD — co-founding a swimming club

Together with my former competitive swimming coach from Nuoto Club Brescia, I helped establish Dinamic Nuoto Vallecamonica ASD.

In the beginning the group was tiny. Some days I was the only athlete showing up to train. We kept going, the team grew, and I gradually took on coaching responsibilities and helped train other swimmers.

I was also involved in training **Giuseppe Romele**, who later earned Paralympic medals and world titles in para cross-country skiing. His sporting success is his own achievement; my contribution was part of an earlier swimming environment.

[Giuseppe Romele — Italian Paralympic Committee profile](https://www.comitatoparalimpico.it/organizzazione/sport/atleti-paralimpici/scheda_atleta/268%3AGiuseppe_Romele.html)

## Giornale della Valcamonica — volunteer journalism

I contributed to a local newspaper as a volunteer journalist, supporting reporting, editing and publication of community news.

These experiences taught me how to keep people engaged, take responsibility without a formal job description and contribute to something larger than myself.
