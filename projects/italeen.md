# Italeen — built and operated an independent food-commerce business

**Live website:** https://italeen.com

**My role:** founder, business operator, product owner and hands-on builder of the commerce stack.

## The problem

Food producers specialize in producing food, not running a unified online storefront. An ordinary e-commerce checkout assumes goods can be fulfilled like a single warehouse order. That assumption breaks when different small producers prepare different perishable products with their own shipping constraints.

## What I built

I founded Italeen and developed its online storefront and operating model. The business connects customers with Italian artisan food producers; Italeen manages customer orders, shipping arrangements and support, while food generally remains with the producer until an order is prepared.

The work has included:

- Building and operating a WooCommerce storefront and a custom GeneratePress child theme.
- Recruiting producers, managing commercial relationships and maintaining the assortment.
- Designing producer-specific shipping and fulfillment flows.
- Developing custom WooCommerce logic for shipping packages, shipping VAT, food comparison prices and operations.
- Running customer acquisition, content, support and the end-to-end logistics coordination.
- Securing Invitalia funding and a sponsored workspace at Kilometro Rosso Innovation District.

## The order journey

1. A customer selects food from one or more producers.
2. The cart identifies producer-specific packages instead of pretending there is one warehouse.
3. WooCommerce and the configured shipping methods calculate commercial prices; dedicated code handles package identity and appropriate shipping-VAT allocation.
4. The producer prepares and packs the food; Italeen arranges labels, transportation and customer support.
5. The customer receives separate shipments where needed.

**Why it matters:** this is a working business process, not a demo designed around a hypothetical customer.
